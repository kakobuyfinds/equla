From discovery to decision

How to use a KakoBuy spreadsheet

Use the directory to discover items, then confirm the exact product and service details before making a payment.

1

Build a shortlist

Filter the catalog, open the source listing and record the item ID, desired color and size.

2

Confirm the option

Check current price, measurements, stock and the original buying link. Confirm that your chosen agent accepts the item.

3

Review the actual item

If using an agent, review warehouse photos when available. Ask about missing measurements before approving shipment.

4

Compare the full total

Include domestic delivery, service charges, international shipping and any applicable import costs in your budget.

What should your shortlist record?

A useful KakoBuy spreadsheet keeps the selected option beside the source link so that similar listings remain easy to distinguish.

  • Identity: item name, CAFANS ID, source URL and date checked.
  • Variant: color, size, quantity and stated materials.
  • Price: original currency, selected-option price and separately quoted charges.
  • Open questions: measurements, availability, return deadline and missing photos.

Check fit before choosing a size

Compare measurements with a similar item you already own. A size label alone does not describe the fit.

  • T-shirts and sweatshirts: compare chest width, body length, shoulders and sleeves.
  • Shoes: consult the listing’s size chart and distinguish foot length from insole length.
  • Photo review: check the selected color, quantity and visible condition.
  • Unclear details: request confirmation; a catalog image does not establish authenticity or material composition.
Plan the complete order

Your price is more than the product.

Enter your own quotes in USD. This worksheet adds the amounts you provide; it does not estimate shipping, duties or agent fees.

Entered-cost subtotal—Enter your quotes to calculate a subtotal.

Leave unknown charges blank. A partial subtotal is not a final delivered price. Currency conversion and payment charges must be included in your own figures where relevant.

Compare shipping quotes fairly

Use the same destination, items and packing assumptions for every quote. Ask which weight basis applies: actual parcel weight, volumetric weight or another line-specific rule.

Confirm tracking, item restrictions, coverage terms and the point from which any transit estimate starts. Obtain the current quote from the service handling your parcel.

KakoBuy spreadsheet FAQ

What is a KakoBuy spreadsheet?

A KakoBuy spreadsheet is a product discovery list used to organize shopping links, categories, prices and buying notes. This page combines a buying guide with a selection imported from the CAFANS catalog.

Where do the product buttons go?

The two main buttons open the CAFANS homepage. Each catalog product link opens its corresponding CAFANS detail page. This page does not process orders.

Are the displayed prices final?

No. Prices are estimated USD amounts copied from CAFANS on November 19, 2026. Confirm the selected variant, current price, fees and shipping before paying.

Do these products have verified QC photos?

This page displays catalog images, not independently verified warehouse QC photographs. Review the actual item photographs and request missing measurements through the service handling your order.

Can I download this directory as a spreadsheet?

This version is a searchable web directory. It does not provide an Excel or Google Sheets download. You can use the source links and item IDs when building your own comparison sheet.

How should I handle a changed or unavailable listing?

Open the CAFANS source page and confirm that it still identifies the item and variant you want. Mark unavailable products in your own shortlist and refresh the quoted price before ordering.
